### 🧠 About me

I'm a **Machine Learning Engineer with a background in Electrical Engineering**, currently specialising in Artificial Intelligence.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **computer vision, deep learning and generative AI**, especially when a model needs to go beyond a notebook and actually become part of a working product.

Outside of work, you'll probably find me **planning my next trip, taking photos of landscapes, or watching a K-drama.** ✈️📷🍿

---

### 🛠️ What I usually work on

👁️ **Computer Vision**

Object detection, segmentation, classification and recognition with models such as YOLO and RT-DETR. From dataset curation and annotation to optimisation and deployment.

🤖 **LLMs & Agents**

Multi-agent workflows, RAG, document intelligence, NER/PII anonymisation and self-hosted LLM inference with tools like LangGraph, Transformers and vLLM.

🚀 **From model to production**

I enjoy the part where research becomes an actual system. Building APIs, containerising models, optimising inference and deploying AI services with FastAPI, Docker and GCP.
